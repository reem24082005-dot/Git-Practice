🌿 About Us – Flora

Rooted in family. Grown by hand. Blooming across Egypt.

Every great story starts with a seed. Ours was planted in 2020, in the heart of Cairo, during a time when the world needed more beauty, more connection, and more meaning. That's when we opened our first small workshop—a family dream built on a simple belief: flowers should do more than just decorate a room. They should nurture the land and the people who grow them.


Today, Flora remains proudly family-owned and family-run. From early morning harvests to the final ribbon tied around your bouquet, our hands never leave the work. We don't outsource. We don't import lifeless stems. Instead, we grow every flower ourselves, and craft every tool by hand, right here in Egypt. You'll feel the difference in the scent of a fresh jasmine sprig, the strength of a hand-forged vase, and the honesty of a petal that never touched a cargo hold.


We currently call Cairo home—one cozy, sunlit location where you can watch arrangements come to life. And because we believe every city deserves a little more green, we are thrilled to announce that a second shop is on its way to Alexandria. Soon, the Mediterranean breeze will carry our blooms, too.


But our roots go deeper than our storefronts. We know that a thriving business must lift up the community that sustains it. That's why 20% of our profits go directly to local farmers—the men and women who work the soil alongside us. When you buy from Flora, you're not just buying flowers. You're investing in Egyptian agriculture, fair practice, and family livelihoods.


From our family to yours—welcome to our garden.
